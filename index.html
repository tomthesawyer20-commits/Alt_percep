Theory

Core Equations

The UGF model is built on three foundational equations linking geometry and force.

Geometric Coupling Function

F_ugf(r) = κ · (l_P / r)ⁿ

κ is the force-specific coupling constant, l_P is the Planck length, and n sets the geometric scale exponent for each force.

Modified Friedmann Equation

H²(z) = H₀² [Ω_m(1+z)³ + Ω_Λ + Ω_geo(1+z)^(4/3)]

Ω_geo ≈ 0.02 encodes geometric curvature in the cosmic expansion history, partially replacing the dark energy contribution.

UGF Gravitational Force

F_grav = G·m₁·m₂/r² · [1 + κ_g·(l_P/r)²]

Classical Newtonian gravity with a Planck-scale correction term that becomes significant only at sub-nuclear distances.

UGF Strong Force

F_s = α_s·ℏc/r² · e^(-r/r_s) · (1 + κ_s)

Yukawa-type confinement with a geometric curvature node amplification at the hadronic scale (r_s ≈ 1 fm).
