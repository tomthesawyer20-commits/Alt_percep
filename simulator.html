How It Works

Simulation Engine

🌌 Force Law

F = (G·mᵢmⱼ − k·qᵢqⱼ) / r^n

A 2-D analogue of the UGF force law. The exponent n controls how quickly force falls off with distance — set by the geometric scale exponent.

⚡ Geometric Coupling

F_ugf = F_base · [1 + κ·(l_P/r)^n]

At short range the Planck-scale curvature correction (κ term) amplifies the force, producing clustering behaviour absent in purely Newtonian dynamics.

🔄 Time Integration

v(t+dt) = v(t)·η + (F/m)·dt

Symplectic Euler integration with a velocity damping factor η to keep the simulation stable. Particles reflect off the canvas boundary.
